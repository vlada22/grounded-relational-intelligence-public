The point of the experiment

Similarity is evidence. A relationship is a claim.

Patch features can support retrieval, grouping, and boundaries. The moment the system says two regions are the same structure, near, adjacent, or semantically related, it has added a new claim. Keeping those steps separate makes both the useful structure and the failures easier to inspect.
