The point of the experiment

A useful representation is not yet a world model.

Patch features can support retrieval, grouping, and boundaries. The moment we say two regions are the same object, adjacent, near, or semantically similar, we have made a new claim. Keeping those steps separate makes the system easier to inspect and the failures easier to understand.
